It starts with how you feel

Tell it how much energy you have. The garden listens.

Each time you open Lumina, it asks one question, and shapes the whole game around your answer. You can change it at any time.

I have some energyPuzzles, planting and exploring, all open.

Somewhere in betweenA lighter island. Hints always on, nothing pressing.

Low, be gentle with meRain falls, the garden tends itself, and you rest.

Rest is part of it

Low day? Just rest.

Most games punish you for stepping away. Lumina was built around it. On a hard day the rain comes in and the garden tends itself while you do nothing at all. Rest even earns its own reward: one island, the Moon Garden, only opens to people who have rested.

There are no streaks to break, no daily goals and no timers. Your island keeps growing whether or not you show up, and nothing wilts, expires or is owed while you are away.

Gentle pastimes

Nothing timed. Nothing failed. Leave any time.

When you have the energy for something more, there are small, unhurried pastimes: wind chimes with no wrong notes, lanterns to light, fireflies that are slow on purpose. None of them can be failed or timed out, and there is always a nudge if you want one.

Nine islands to find

A sky full of places to grow things

Plant and tend your first island, then find more as the garden grows. Each one has its own weather, its own plants and its own quiet, and visitors wander in, from a garden snail to the fireflies.

The Cloud Garden

Your first island. Open sky, slow weather, a long way down.

The Orchard Shelf

A terrace of old fruit trees at the golden end of the day. Windfalls in the grass, wasps too lazy to bother you.

The Reed Bank

Low water and tall reeds, and the sound of both. Herons stand in it. Frogs run the place.

The Windmill Rise

A hill of long grass with a slow mill on top. The sails turn whether or not there is wind, which nobody has explained.

The Moon Garden

An island that only opens to people who have rested. Silver grass, a well of light, and an owl who keeps her own hours.

The Alpine Retreat

A mountain shoulder above treeline. Wind in the pines, snow that never settles hard, a stream you can hear from anywhere.

THE FAR SKY PACK

The Crystal Caverns

Underground and very dark by design, for light-sensitive days. Geodes throw a low glow.

THE FAR SKY PACK

The Tidal Shallows

A sandbar at the low end of the afternoon. Warm shallow water on three sides and nothing on the horizon.

THE WARM WATERS PACK

The Hot Spring Terraces

Mineral pools stepping down a warm hillside, steam standing in the air. Built to feel like heat on a bad joint.

Comfort, your way

Set it however your body wants it

Lumina was made for people living with chronic illness, pain, fatigue or migraine, so comfort is never buried three menus deep.

  • Low light, right now: one tap dims everything at once
  • Warmth over the screen, and colour themes including high contrast
  • Softened high sounds, or no sound at all
  • Movement turned off for sensitive eyes
  • Bigger buttons and a left-handed layout

Questions

Before you plant

Who is Lumina for?

It was made for people living with chronic illness, pain, fatigue or migraine, and for anyone who wants a game that asks very little of them.

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. Rare plant varieties and two island packs are optional one-time purchases, and there are no subscriptions.

What happens if I don't play for a while?

Nothing bad. Nothing wilts and nothing is owed, and the garden keeps growing while you are away.

The screen is too bright, or the sound too sharp.

Open Comfort. Low light, right now dims everything at once, and you can warm the screen, soften high sounds, turn off movement and make buttons bigger.
